PetMate helps pet owners find breeding partners for their pets nearby. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you control it.

## 1. What we collect

| Data                                                                                                                       | Why we need it                                     | Who can see it                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name, email, profile photo (from your Google account)                                                                      | To create your account and show who you are        | Name and photo: other signed-in users. Email: only you                                                           |
| City and short bio (optional)                                                                                              | To show where you are based                        | Other signed-in users                                                                                            |
| Phone number (you enter it; we do not verify it)                                                                           | So an owner you agree to meet can contact you      | **Only the other owner, and only after one of you accepts a request between your pets.** Never shown on listings |
| Pet details: name, species, breed, gender, date of birth, photos, description, vaccination and vet information, stud terms | To list your pet and match it with compatible pets | Other signed-in users, when the listing is active                                                                |
| Approximate location of your pet                                                                                           | To show pets near each other                       | Other users see only an **approximate area (about 500 m)**. We never store your exact address or coordinates     |
| Health or pedigree document photo (optional)                                                                               | To review a "verified" badge                       | **Only you and PetMate staff.** Stored privately and never shown to other users                                  |
| Match requests, messages, ratings, reports                                                                                 | To run requests and keep the community safe        | Participants in a request; ratings are shown on your profile; reports are seen only by PetMate staff             |
| Saved (favorite) pets                                                                                                      | To remember pets you like                          | Only you                                                                                                         |
| Notification token                                                                                                         | To send you alerts about requests                  | Only our systems                                                                                                 |

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

## 2. How we use it

To run the app (accounts, listings, search, requests, notifications), to keep it safe (limits, reports, review of listings), to improve reliability, and to meet legal duties.

## 3. Location

We ask for your device location only when you add a pet or search nearby. Your pet's location is rounded to a grid of about 500 m before it is stored. You can refuse permission and enter a city instead.

## 4. Who we share it with (service providers)

- **Supabase**: database, login and file storage.
- **Google**: sign-in with Google.
- **Cloudinary**: hosting of pet photos.
- **Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging**: delivery of push notifications.

They process data on our behalf. Some of them store data outside India.

## 5. How long we keep it

While your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, pets, requests, ratings and saved pets are deleted from our database. Copies in backups are removed on the normal backup cycle. ## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Edit** your name, city and phone in Profile.
- **Delete your account** in Profile → Delete account. This is permanent.
- You may ask us to **access, correct or erase** your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL].
- You can turn off notifications and location in your phone settings.

## 7. Children

PetMate is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is limited by database rules so users can read only what is meant to be shared. No system is perfectly secure.
